#+TITLE: Influence, Promotion, and Cognitive Allocation
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Influential NPCs may be promoted to or demoted from persistent LLM access dynamically.
- Identity, personal history, memories, and plans survive changes.
- Social rank alone is insufficient to define importance.

* PROPOSED
- Manage a fixed concurrent inference budget, including temporary dialogue access.
- Consider political reach, resources, unusual potential, magical research, ambition, and decision complexity when promoting.
- Demotion does not erase any character data.

* OPEN
- Promotion criteria, fairness, capacity limit, preemption, intergenerational redistribution.
