#+TITLE: NPC Traits and Attributes
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- NPCs retain identity and distinguishing characteristics even without ongoing LLM access.
- Traits and skills affect influence and social opportunities; magical potential is not the same as political rank.

* PROPOSED
- Track caution, curiosity, ambition, loyalty, risk tolerance, skills, health, social ability, magical aptitude, mana capacity, absorption, output, background, wealth, rank.
- Allow learning, aging, training, environmental effects, and possibly heredity.

* OPEN
- Trait scales, heredity model, learning speed, aging, distributions.
