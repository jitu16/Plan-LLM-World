#+TITLE: NPC Decision Systems
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Conventional AI handles basic behaviors for every NPC, including influential NPCs.
- A limited subset have persistent access to local-LLM reasoning for major decisions and social behavior.
- Ordinary NPCs must temporarily receive LLM dialogue when speaking with influential characters; they should not seem robotic.

* PROPOSED
- Select the controller by situation, not by NPC identity.
- Validate all model-suggested actions in the game engine.
- In combat, conventional reactions and precomputed plans continue without synchronous inference.
- Social utterances and strategy proposals do not directly edit world state.

* OPEN
- Other temporary LLM triggers, fallback policies, utility AI versus behavior trees/GOAP, dialogue budgets.
