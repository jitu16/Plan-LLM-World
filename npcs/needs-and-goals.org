#+TITLE: NPC Needs, Goals, and Intentions
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- NPCs can originate personal projects and unusual world-changing plans through valid actions.
- Ordinary AI maintains routines and executes currently adopted plans.

* PROPOSED
- Use basic needs (food, shelter, safety, relationships) and short/medium/long-term goals.
- Plans record steps, resources, priorities, conditional branches, and cancellation rules.
- Personality and memory influence decisions instead of scripted plot triggers.

* OPEN
- Priority resolution, need decay, commitment and reconsideration.
