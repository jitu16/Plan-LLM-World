#+TITLE: Relationships, Reputation, and Lineage
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Influence can emerge from social encounters and family lines across generations.
- Conversations with powerful NPCs can enable ordinary characters to rise.

* PROPOSED
- Track trust, familiarity, obligations, resentment, alliances, rivalries, kinship, reputation, and mentorship.
- Update relations from perceived events; inherit property or status only under explicit legal/social rules.

* OPEN
- Family formation, marriage, inheritance, kinship depth, dynamics.
