#+TITLE: Knowledge, Beliefs, and Information Limits
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- NPCs do not have access to omniscient world state.
- NPCs may hold false, incomplete, disputed, or obsolete beliefs.
- Magical discoveries belong to discoverers until transmitted.

* PROPOSED
- Attach source, confidence, observation date, and evidence to important beliefs.
- Rumors can change beliefs without modifying facts.
- LLM prompts should expose only an NPC's observations, knowledge, and plausible inference.

* OPEN
- Perception, unreliable reports, rumor propagation, epistemic constraints.
