#+TITLE: Architecture Decision Log
#+STARTUP: overview

* Conventions
- AGREED :: Explicitly selected design direction; technical details can remain open.
- PROPOSED :: Candidate implementation, not yet approved.
- OPEN :: Unsettled question; no assumptions should silently become facts.
- REVISION :: Record superseded decisions with their reasons.

* AGREED: Autonomous medieval-fantasy simulation
- The world should evolve independently; player participation is optional.
- Civilization develops over multiple generations.
- NPCs and institutions may attempt unforeseen goals, including conspiracies and coups, but only through valid game-engine actions.
- Basic hard magic is present from the beginning.

* AGREED: Genesis
- Start with approximately 50 NPCs inside a shield of finite radius created by a special artifact.
- The exterior is extremely monster-infested; millions or billions of monsters may be represented abstractly rather than individually simulated.
- Monsters can be instantiated at encounters, with distance from the protective shield potentially influencing risk.
- The population can grow, develop settlements, and explore; simulation speed must be adjustable.
- The starting region graph has one Genesis zone with six adjacent unexplored zones.

* AGREED: NPC cognitive architecture
- Most NPCs use conventional game AI for routine behavior.
- A bounded, dynamically changing subset receives ongoing local-LLM access for consequential decisions, long-term planning, and dialogue.
- Ordinary NPCs receive temporary LLM access when conversing with influential NPCs, so they do not appear robotic.
- Promotion and demotion must preserve the NPC's identity, memories, relationships, and plans.
- Conventional AI continues to handle immediate movement, combat, and existing plans for all NPCs.

* AGREED: Simulation authority
- The world engine is authoritative for facts, resources, success and failure, and the outcomes of actions.
- LLMs propose goals, decisions, plans, speech, and research experiments; they do not change reality by narration.

* AGREED: Hard magic
- Except for specially declared artifacts, magic follows an energy-conserving ruleset.
- NPCs absorb mana from their environment; biological metabolism is not the source of mana regeneration.
- Local mana reserves are finite and replenish at region-specific rates.
- Individual attributes include mana storage capacity and absorption ability; output/power limits are candidates.
- Every magic symbol has defined executable semantics. NPCs can experimentally discover combinations and create unanticipated spells.
- The magic engine validates and executes inscriptions subject to mana, materials, permissions, and physical rules.
- Mana may produce fire, water-related effects, and other selected elemental phenomena, subject to matter/energy accounting.
- Grimoires, inscriptions, and mana stones are candidate carriers of magic programs.

* AGREED: Zone-based mana transport
- The six initially unexplored neighboring zones each have finite mana reservoirs, not fixed-density boundary reservoirs.
- Inter-zone mana flow subtracts mana from the donating region and adds it to the receiving region, conservatively.
- Discrete zone-by-zone exchanges are preferred over continuous, fine-grained diffusion simulation.

* AGREED: Special artifacts and shields
- The Genesis shield is powered by a divine or otherwise exceptional artifact with a potentially inexhaustible mana supply and potentially extraordinary durability.
- Conventional shields can be damaged, replenished by mana injection, and eventually broken.
- Shield-specific keys and wearable passage artifacts with limited passage radius are desired concepts.
- Literal infinity versus long-lived or externally supplied divine energy is not settled.

* AGREED: Spherical world layout
- Use a sphere tiled mostly by hexagonal zones, plus twelve pentagonal zones, constructed as the dual of an icosahedral subdivision.
- Most cells have six neighbors, twelve pentagonal cells have five; Genesis can use one of the ordinary hexagonal cells.
- Spherical topology means there are no external map edges; purely periodic hex grids would instead have toroidal topology.
- Update local mana several times per simulated day (initial candidate: 3-4 times), using symmetric pairwise flows and nonnegative reserve constraints.

* PROPOSED: Timing
- Simulated time, NPC action duration, and model computation time are distinct.
- In autonomous Genesis, world time may pause at consequential decision barriers while LLMs finish.
- Interactive play may require explicit strategic decision pauses rather than silent loss of decision quality.
- Every combatant continues to have ordinary executable behavior even when inference is delayed.

* OPEN: Foundational questions
- Determine spherical grid resolution and how geography is generated beyond the seven Genesis zones.
- Source/accounting of ambient mana replenishment and divine exceptions to conservation.
- Numerical diffusion coefficients and stable update rates.
- Shield duration, durability, key semantics, codebreaking, and passage radius rules.
- How background population and monsters are abstracted and later instantiated consistently.
- How many influential NPCs can be supported and how decisions are scheduled in player-visible time.
- Symbol grammar, mana-to-matter rules, and experimental feedback.
