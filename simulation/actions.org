#+TITLE: Actions, Validation, and Execution
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- NPCs can devise novel goals but can only change reality through actions the engine supports.
- LLM output is a proposal or intention, not an authoritative outcome.

* PROPOSED
- Actions include actor, required resources, target, authority, preconditions, time, and effect type.
- Validate before and during execution, charge resources, record results, and emit observable events.
- Allow commanders to issue objectives and conditional orders implemented through lower-level actions.

* OPEN
- Composable action set, simultaneous conflicts, partial results, cancellation.
