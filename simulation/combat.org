#+TITLE: Combat, Armies, and Strategy
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- LLM commanders issue strategy and high-level orders; conventional AI handles soldiers, unit movement, formations, targeting, morale, and immediate defense.
- No character must freeze during LLM latency.
- Archers, pikemen, cavalry, and spellcasters should not each need an LLM.

* PROPOSED
- Use formation/unit-level simulation; instantiate detailed actors only when necessary.
- Prepare orders and contingencies before battle and revisit plans at strategic decision points.
- Apply magical attacks only through validated spells with paid costs.

* OPEN
- Battle timing, command delivery, morale, fog of war, unit detail, decision pauses.
