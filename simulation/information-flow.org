#+TITLE: Information Propagation
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Knowledge does not instantly become universal when a single NPC discovers it.
- Conversations and institutions are mechanisms for social influence.

* PROPOSED
- Transmit beliefs, rumors, magic, and news through conversations, travel, messages, teachers, grimoires, officials, and trade.
- Track source credibility independently of objective truth.
- Use only accessible facts when constructing an LLM interaction.

* OPEN
- Communication delays, secrecy, literacy, censorship, misinformation.
