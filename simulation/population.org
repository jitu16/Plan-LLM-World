#+TITLE: Population, Generations, and Cohorts
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- The world develops from approximately 50 Genesis NPCs over many generations.
- Large populations can be represented without making every person a full-time simulated individual.

* PROPOSED
- Begin by instantiating Genesis people individually.
- Later use population cohorts for births, deaths, farming, labor, migration, and reproduction at appropriate fidelity.
- Materialize particular individuals with histories consistent with existing aggregate records.
- Maintain explicit lineage and inheritance for important families.

* OPEN
- Demographic rules, threshold for cohorting, aging, materialization consistency.
