#+TITLE: Data Structures
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- An NPC's persistent identity, memory, and relationships are independent of its cognitive controller.

* PROPOSED
- World: clock, map, regions, local mana, event queue, rules version.
- Cohort: location, head count, age distribution, production, migration.
- NPC: immutable ID, lineage, traits, resources, needs, abilities, goals, cognition tier.
- Memory: owner, event, time, viewpoint, source, confidence, salience.
- Institution: members, territory, hierarchy, assets, policy, conflicts.
- Action: actor, target, preconditions, resource cost, time, effect, outcome.
- Artifact: magical rules, source of mana, keys, radius, durability.
- Spell: typed instruction sequence, compiled form, cost, provenance.

* OPEN
- Concrete schemas, relational versus entity-component design, indexes, snapshots, event sourcing.
