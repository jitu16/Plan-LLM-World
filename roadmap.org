#+TITLE: Development Roadmap
#+STARTUP: overview

* Objective
Build a persistent, autonomous medieval-fantasy world that can develop for many generations whether or not a player joins. The primary goal is causal consistency and emergent society, not scripted narratives.

* Stage 0: Planning [CURRENT]
- Define world rules, NPC cognition/memory, simulation scheduling, artifacts, and magic.
- Mark settled choices and unresolved questions separately in [[file:design-decisions.org][design-decisions.org]].

* Stage 1: Genesis prototype [PROPOSED]
- Approximately 50 individual NPCs in an artifact-shielded settlement.
- World clock, needs, resources, ordinary AI, identity, persistence, and memories.
- Hostile exterior with abstract monster pressure.

* Stage 2: LLM-enabled society [PROPOSED]
- A bounded pool of influential NPCs with distinct contexts/memories using shared LLM infrastructure.
- Temporary local-LLM dialogue for ordinary NPCs who interact with influential NPCs.
- Promotion and demotion without loss of identity or memory.

* Stage 3: Magic and generations [PROPOSED]
- Regional finite mana reservoirs, conservation, executable symbols, magic research, and artifacts.
- Births, deaths, inheritance, knowledge propagation, institutional growth, new settlements.

* Stage 4: Warfare and kingdoms [PROPOSED]
- Conventional AI for combat; commanders' LLMs issue valid strategic orders.
- Courts, noble houses, kingdoms, commerce, diplomacy, and large statistical populations.

* Stage 5: Optional interactive RPG [PROPOSED]
- Player participates without being essential to world evolution.
- Test explicit strategic decision boundaries and their effects on perceived responsiveness.

* OPEN: Exit criteria
- Specify benchmarks for simulated years/hour, latency, memory fidelity, conservation, battle continuity, and reproducibility.
