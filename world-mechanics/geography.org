#+TITLE: Geography and Territory
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Genesis settlement has a protected radius and a hostile outside.
- Environmental mana quantity and replenishment vary geographically.

* PROPOSED
- Track terrain, water, farmland, travel difficulty, biome, danger, shield boundary, and territorial claims separately.
- Expansion follows from available resources and security, rather than a permanent invisible wall.

* OPEN
- Map-generation rules, extent, spatial resolution, geographical correlation with magic.
