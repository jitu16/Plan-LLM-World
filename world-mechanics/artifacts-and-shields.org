#+TITLE: Artifacts, Shields, and Access Keys
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Genesis uses a divine or exceptional shield artifact, possibly with an inexhaustible source of mana or exceptional durability.
- Ordinary shields can lose durability under attack, recover with injected mana, and break.
- Shields may be tagged with unique access keys; compatible wearable artifacts can confer passage, potentially within a radius.

* PROPOSED
- Separate shield geometry, durability, mana expenditure, and permissions.
- A passage artifact may open a localized portal or grant conditional crossing access, subject to engine validation.
- Treat infinite durability or mana as explicit world exceptions; avoid silently violating ordinary accounting.

* OPEN
- Is the divine shield actually infinite, externally supplied, or protected only until a trigger?
- Are shield keys unforgeable, or can magic researchers decipher or counterfeit them?
- How is an aura of passage applied to companions, carried items, or enemies?
