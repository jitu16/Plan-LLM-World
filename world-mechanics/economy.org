#+TITLE: Economy and Production
#+STARTUP: overview

* PROPOSED
- Represent food, labor, land, raw materials, tools, agriculture, transport, scarcity, trade, taxes, and storage.
- Institutions and NPCs can formulate economic objectives, but production requires actual inputs.
- Use aggregated routine transactions where full detail is unnecessary.
- Magic can change productivity only through accounted energy/material costs.

* OPEN
- Currency, prices, exchange mechanisms, property, labor markets, spoilage, social classes.
