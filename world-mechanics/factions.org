#+TITLE: Political Institutions and Factions
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- NPCs may pursue novel political plans, including conspiracies, without a hard-coded story scenario.
- Political authority and the need for LLM planning are related but not identical.

* PROPOSED
- Represent courts, dynasties, councils, armies, noble houses, guilds, kingdoms, and alliances as entities with members and resources.
- Actions alter legitimacy, obedience, relationships, territories, institutions, and conflict through normal simulation rules.

* OPEN
- Genesis governance, inheritance law, legitimacy, diplomacy, succession, command authority.
