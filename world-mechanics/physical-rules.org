#+TITLE: Physical and Magical Conservation Laws
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Ordinary magic converts stored or ambient mana energy into physical effects while conserving energy.
- The game engine, not the LLM, calculates physical validity.
- Divine artifacts can be declared explicit exceptions or linked to an external source.

* PROPOSED
- Account for heat, motion, force, elemental phenomena, inefficiency, spell power, matter transport, and materials.
- Water manipulation may move or condense existing water; creating matter requires explicit mass-energy rules.
- Document divine mana carefully rather than pretending it is an ordinary finite reservoir.

* OPEN
- Physical units, elemental list, loss mechanisms, matter creation, divine exceptions.
