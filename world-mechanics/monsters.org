#+TITLE: Monsters and External Threat
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Genesis territory outside the shield is densely populated with monsters.
- The world can conceptually contain millions or billions of monsters without instantiating each one.
- Risk can vary with distance from the shield.

* PROPOSED
- Maintain regional monster density, population pressure, movement, habitat, and threat level.
- Generate actual monsters for patrols, observations, raids, and fights while respecting observed territory and physical consistency.

* OPEN
- Monster species, ecology, reproduction, movement, source, strength distribution, shield attacks.
