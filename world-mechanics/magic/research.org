#+TITLE: Magical Discovery and Research
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- NPCs independently experiment with magic and may discover new combinations and new-to-them principles.
- LLM proposes hypotheses; the game engine determines experimental effects and observable evidence.

* PROPOSED
- Separate true physics from symbol knowledge, theories, experimental records, and institutional teaching.
- Experiments cost materials, time, and mana, and can fail or cause danger under explicit rules.
- Knowledge may spread through grimoires, mentorship, commerce, espionage, and travel.
- Researchers can be intellectually gifted without being powerful mana users.

* OPEN
- How symbols are first found, feedback visibility, risks, research difficulty, knowledge transfer.
