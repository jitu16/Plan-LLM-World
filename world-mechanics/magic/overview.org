#+TITLE: Hard Magic System
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Include limited hard magic from Genesis onward.
- Ordinary magic obeys finite environmental mana and the world's conservation laws.
- NPCs can discover new spells using sequences of symbols with defined executable semantics.
- The world engine knows magic rules even when NPCs do not.
- Once validated, a spell can be used by ordinary NPCs without LLM inference.

* PROPOSED
- Separate [[file:mana.org][mana reservoirs]], [[file:symbols-and-spells.org][symbolic runtime]], [[file:research.org][experimentation]], and [[file:../artifacts-and-shields.org][artifacts]].

* OPEN
- Core symbol set, elemental effects, skill requirements, spell safety, failure modes.
