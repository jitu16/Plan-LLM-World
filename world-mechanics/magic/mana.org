#+TITLE: Environmental Mana Reservoirs
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Mana is drawn from the environment, not regenerated from an NPC's metabolic energy.
- Each region has a finite local mana reservoir and a replenishment rate that may differ geographically.
- NPCs have individual mana storage and absorption capacity.

* PROPOSED
- For region r track available M_r(t), maximum capacity, inflow, replenishment, and transfer to neighbors.
- For NPC i track current mana, storage limit, absorption rate, and possible maximum discharge rate.
- Require accounting for extraction and replenishment so transfer does not duplicate mana.

* OPEN
- Spatial transport and diffusion, external replenishment source, leakage, saturation, divine artifacts.
