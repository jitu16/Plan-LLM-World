#+TITLE: Magic Symbols and Executable Spells
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Every symbol has a fixed engine-defined meaning; symbolic combinations are interpreted as programs.
- New inscriptions succeed or fail on syntax, semantics, mana, material resources, preconditions, and physical laws.
- NPCs can combine symbols into spells not explicitly designed as spell names by developers.
- Grimoires, mana stones, and inscriptions may store or power programs.

* PROPOSED
- Typed operations and parameters; parse, type-check, validate preconditions and energy, then execute within cost and instruction budgets.
- Cache/compile validated spells for use by ordinary NPCs and battle units without fresh inference.
- Restrict recursion, unbounded loops, and computationally explosive spell programs.

* OPEN
- Symbol alphabet and grammar, runtime semantics, experimental error feedback, timing, inscription durability, matter creation versus manipulation.
