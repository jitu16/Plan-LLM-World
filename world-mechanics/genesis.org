#+TITLE: Genesis Scenario
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Begin with approximately 50 NPCs.
- A special artifact creates a protective shield with a finite spatial radius.
- The exterior is inhabited by vast monster populations and is dangerous to traverse.
- Generational growth, social change, and eventual expansion are desired.

* PROPOSED
- Allow shielded survival, agriculture, knowledge growth, institutions, and families.
- Give some explorers passage through shield-keyed wearable artifacts.
- Track distant monsters abstractly, instantiating only those relevant to local encounters.

* OPEN
- Origin of the initial population; starting ages, tools, knowledge and resources; shield radius; expansion conditions.
