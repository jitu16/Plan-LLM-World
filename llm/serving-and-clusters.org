#+TITLE: Inference Serving and Clusters
#+STARTUP: overview

* AGREED
- Local model inference may scale across multiple Mac mini-class machines.
- Restrict the number of permanently LLM-assisted NPCs to a reliable compute budget.

* PROPOSED
- Prefer multiple independent serving workers for parallel NPC requests over distributing one model among machines unless benchmarking suggests otherwise.
- Keep canonical world state and decisions in a centralized scheduler with model workers receiving task snapshots.
- Prioritize active social interactions, separate research from latency-sensitive dialogue, and monitor queues.

* OPEN
- Hardware model/RAM, capacity, batching, network latency, failover, scheduling fairness.
